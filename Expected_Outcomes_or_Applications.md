# Expected Outcomes or Applications

## Project: BuildBot

BuildBot is an AI-powered recommendation platform designed to help online PC parts retailers provide personalized product suggestions to customers. The system allows users to enter their budget and purpose, after which the platform generates three customized PC build recommendations based on the store’s actual product catalog. This project is expected to bring practical and academic value in the fields of artificial intelligence, e-commerce, and web-based software development.

## Expected Outcomes

The expected outcomes of this project include the development of a smart and user-friendly recommendation system that improves the online shopping experience for customers. By using AI, the system can reduce confusion and decision fatigue for users who often struggle to choose compatible PC components. It is also expected to increase customer engagement, improve conversion rates, and help retailers present their products more effectively.

Another important outcome is the creation of a scalable and practical software solution that can be used by real businesses. The project combines AI recommendations, a dashboard for store owners, an admin panel, analytics, and a WordPress/WooCommerce plugin, making it a complete digital solution for e-commerce businesses. This demonstrates how modern AI technologies can be applied to solve real-world retail problems.

## Applications

BuildBot has several practical applications in the real world. It can be used by online PC component stores to guide customers in selecting suitable builds according to budget and purpose. It can also be used by local computer shops that want to sell products online with smarter customer support and product recommendations.

In addition, the platform can be extended to other industries such as gaming hardware, laptops, custom-built systems, and other product categories where customers need personalized recommendations. The project also shows strong potential for commercialization as a SaaS-based solution, where multiple stores can use the system through subscriptions and plugin integration.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, BuildBot is expected to provide a valuable solution for both customers and retailers by making product selection easier, faster, and more intelligent. Its applications are not limited to one store or one platform, but can be expanded into a broader AI-driven e-commerce recommendation system for future use.
