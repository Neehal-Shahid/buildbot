FAQ

Questions Answered

Do I need a developer to install BuildBot?

No. You get a single line of code to paste on your website. If you can add a Facebook Pixel or Google Analytics, you can add BuildBot.

What if I don't have a website — only a WooCommerce store?

BuildBot has a WordPress plugin that connects directly to WooCommerce. Your products sync automatically, and the widget appears on your store with one click.

Does the AI recommend products from other stores?

Never. BuildBot only recommends products from your catalog that are marked in-stock. Your customers stay on your store.

How do I pay?

We accept JazzCash and EasyPaisa. After sending payment, you submit your transaction ID in the dashboard. We manually verify and activate your plan within 24 hours.

What happens after my 14-day trial?

Your widget will stop generating recommendations until you subscribe to a paid plan. All your settings, products, and data are saved. You pick up exactly where you left off.
